
# Rescue Sky | AI-Powered Rescue Flight Simulator

> An AI-powered arcade rescue flight simulator built with Google AI Studio and Gemini, featuring AI-generated rescue missions, tactical mission control, and personalized flight debriefs.

## Overview

**Rescue Sky (SKYRESCUE)** is an interactive arcade-style rescue flight game that puts players in the role of a rescue pilot navigating challenging environments to deliver emergency supplies to people affected by disasters.

Built for **GDG Live Pakistan DevFest 2026**, the project combines arcade gameplay with generative AI to make rescue missions more dynamic, engaging, and interactive.

Players navigate their aircraft through dangerous weather conditions, collect relief supplies, manage their aircraft's health, and complete rescue objectives. Gemini AI enhances the experience by generating mission briefings and providing personalized flight debriefs.

## Mission

**Monsoon Peak Relief**

Fly through challenging conditions, collect emergency supplies, and complete your rescue mission while managing distance, health, and your flight performance.

## Features

- **Interactive Flight Gameplay** — Pilot an aircraft through challenging environments.
- **AI Mission Generation** — Use Gemini to generate dynamic rescue mission briefings.
- **Tactical Mission Control** — Approach rescue objectives strategically while navigating hazards.
- **Supply Collection** — Collect essential emergency supplies during your flight.
- **Health & Performance Tracking** — Monitor aircraft health, distance, and score.
- **Pause & Resume** — Pause gameplay and continue your mission when ready.
- **AI Flight Debrief** — Receive AI-generated feedback and mission performance insights.

## Gameplay Loop

1. Receive an AI-generated rescue mission.
2. Launch the rescue flight.
3. Navigate the environment and avoid obstacles.
4. Collect emergency supplies.
5. Manage aircraft health and flight progress.
6. Complete the mission.
7. Review your AI-powered flight debrief.

## Purpose

Rescue Sky is an experimental project exploring how generative AI can enhance interactive gaming experiences while introducing players to the challenges of emergency response and disaster-relief logistics.

It demonstrates how AI can move beyond conventional chat interfaces and become part of an interactive, goal-driven digital experience.

> This project is an educational arcade simulation. It does not represent a real-world emergency response or rescue coordination system.
